Gesso & the Ghost Dogs

A plaster figure and two ghost dogs cross a painted plain where old machines still roll and ships fly the same loops overhead. Gather the green seams, read the eight plates, and find the four dogs still lost out there. When ink rises out of the plain, whitewash it with gesso.

How to play
WASD · Shift
Walk, and hold Shift to run. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
Space
Whistle. Your dogs run off and point at the nearest seams, or in a fight go for the ink.
Right mouse · click
Aim over the shoulder and throw gesso. F throws at the nearest ink.
Q
Still Life. Time nearly stops: sweep the sights across the ink to mark it in red chalk, then click or press Q and every throw lands.
E
Read a plate, talk, call a lost dog, pet a dog.
